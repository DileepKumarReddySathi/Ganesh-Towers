Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are quick answers regarding rent, ownership, documentation, and amenities.

Are the flats available for immediate possession / move-in?
Yes! We have ready-to-move-in flats available for both tenants looking for immediate rental accommodation and buyers seeking immediate home ownership. Contact our management directly to schedule a walkthrough.
Are all the apartments 100% Vastu compliant?
Yes, every unit at Ganesh Towers has been engineered strictly according to Vedic Vastu guidelines. All main entrances are East or North facing, kitchens are placed in Agni moola (South-East), and master bedrooms are placed in South-West.
What are the security deposit and rent terms for rental units?
Rental deposits and monthly rental charges depend on the selected configuration (2 BHK or 3 BHK). Standard agreements are created with direct owner communication, transparent maintenance terms, and zero brokerage.
Can I get a bank loan for purchasing a flat here?
Yes, Ganesh Towers possesses 100% clear titles, approved building plans, and legal clearances. Properties are eligible for home loans through leading nationalized and private banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, etc.).
What provisions exist for car parking and elevators?
We provide covered ground/stilt parking for four-wheelers and two-wheelers, along with a modern automatic elevator equipped with an automatic rescue system and generator connectivity.
